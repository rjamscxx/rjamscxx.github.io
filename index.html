In use · private client system
SINGEL IPMS

Tracks what each project needs, what was bought, what arrived, where it sits and where it went. Every step is its own numbered record, so any figure on any screen traces back to the documents behind it.

  • Stock figures are calculated from movement records, never stored, so they can't drift.
  • Nothing is edited or deleted. Corrections are voided and reversed, and the database enforces it.
  • Whoever raises a document can't approve or receive it, checked by the server and by row-level security.
  • Printable POs, RFQs, receipts and delivery orders, plus spreadsheet imports and exports.

About me

I'm Robert James Cabansay. I started coding to manage my own small business, because the notebook wasn't cutting it. That sales tracker became Checkout Hub PH, and I haven't stopped building since.

I've shipped 16+ production apps for a manufacturing group, restaurants, retail brands, resorts and service shops. I work mostly in Next.js, React, TypeScript, PostgreSQL and Supabase, and build offline-first PWAs when that fits the owner's day better. Projects are tested with Playwright and Vitest and handed over in the client's own accounts.

Before development I spent five years in real estate sales, financial advisory, HR sourcing and customer service, so I build for how owners actually work. Strukkd Labs is my software studio. I'm open to full-time, contract and freelance roles.
